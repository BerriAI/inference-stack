Open the launcher:

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/inference-stack-launcher 8080:8080
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-litellm 4000:4000
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/inference-stack-grafana 3000:80

Then visit http://localhost:8080 and click "Add model".
